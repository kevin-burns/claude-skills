# Sandra's answers — session 2

*(Answers to the questions left open at the end of session 1.)*

**On the stock system rollout:**

"So the vendor's project lead left about six weeks in and they didn't replace him properly — we got a junior who'd never done an implementation. It basically stopped. I ended up running the whole thing: I rebuilt the migration plan, chased their team weekly, ran the data cleansing on our side because their spec was wrong about how our SKUs were structured, and did the training for the warehouse staff myself.

It went live about three months later than the original date, but honestly if I hadn't picked it up I think it would have been abandoned — there was a conversation at board level about pulling out of the contract. My MD said afterwards it would have been written off.

Cost? It was a big system for us. I want to say the contract was around £90,000 but I'd have to check that. The stock discrepancies dropped a lot afterwards — we used to write off a fair amount every quarter and that mostly went away. Maybe 20%? I honestly don't have the number, that came from the finance side."

**On the induction process:**

"I've never counted. We take on maybe 15-20 people a year across both sites, and it's been running since 2017, so you can do the maths. Nobody's ever measured whether it works, but I know the complaints stopped, because they used to come to me."

**On the health and safety thing:**

"I sort of inherited it. I do the annual audit, I keep the risk assessments updated, I'm the one who deals with the auditors when they come. We've passed every year I've done it. I did the IOSH Managing Safely course in 2021 — the company paid for it. I don't really think of it as part of my job, it's just something I ended up owning."

**On the two assistants:**

"They're good. One of them came in with no office experience at all and I trained her up from scratch — she now does the whole supplier ordering side without me. I suppose that's something. The other one I inherited."

**On something she's quietly proud of:**

"Probably that the place doesn't fall over when I'm on holiday any more. That took years to get to. When I started here everything came through me and it was a nightmare — now most of it's documented and other people can pick it up."
