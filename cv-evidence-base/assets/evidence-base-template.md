## Evidence

One entry per claim. Keep the person's own words where they're vivid — they
usually phrase it better under questioning than they did on the CV.

### [Short claim]

- **Context:** [where, when, what situation]
- **What they did:** [specifically theirs, distinguished from the team's]
- **Scale:** [numbers — or "unknown" if not established]
- **Outcome:** [what changed, against what baseline]
- **How they know:** [dashboard, report, colleague who'd confirm, memory]
- **Confidence:** confirmed / approximate / unverified
- **Supports:** [archetypes this is evidence for]
- **Source:** session [n]

Confidence is load-bearing, so treat it strictly:

- **confirmed** — they're certain and could defend it under follow-up questions
- **approximate** — a good-faith estimate; phrase with the hedge intact
- **unverified** — they believe it but would need to check; never goes into CV
  prose without the uncertainty visible
