# Volunteer rota for a food bank

## Problem

The food bank's coordinator fills each day's shifts by phoning volunteers, and finds out a shift is short only when people fail to arrive.

## Who it is for

The coordinator first, and the volunteers who sign up for shifts.

## Goal

Volunteers sign up for food-bank shifts and drop out of them online, and the coordinator sees each day's rota without phoning round.

## Non-goals

- No payments or donations.
- No warehouse stock tracking.

## Anti-goal

A shift that falls below its minimum number of volunteers without the coordinator knowing counts as a failure.

## Success check

For a month, the coordinator fills the rota without phoning anyone.

## Risks

- Some volunteers do not use email often and may miss messages.

## Assumptions

- Assumption: volunteers already have an email address the food bank holds.
