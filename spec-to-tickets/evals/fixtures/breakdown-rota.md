# Rota breakdown (earlier session)

1. **Shift management**: volunteers sign up for, drop out of and swap shifts. Priority: Must. Size: L.
2. **Rota views**: the coordinator's day view and week view. Priority: Must. Size: M.
3. **Under-staffing alerts**: email the coordinator when a shift falls below its minimum. Priority: Must. Size: M.
4. **Reminders**: email volunteers the day before their shift. Priority: Should. Size: M.
5. **Spreadsheet export** of the rota. Priority: Could. Size: S.
